# Aestheva SEO keyword plan (Thiruvananthapuram)

Researched October 2026. Exact monthly volumes need Google Ads Keyword Planner or Search Console data; this plan ranks
keywords by intent and by how weak the current results are. Review it against Search Console after 6–8 weeks.

## What we found
- **Who ranks today:** directories, not booking sites. WhatClinic (dentists, whitening, laser, by city), WedMeGood and
  WeddingWire (bridal makeup), ThreeBestRated (tattoo, massage), Justdial and Practo, plus individual clinic websites.
- **The gap:** almost nobody publishes real Trivandrum prices. "Price/cost in Trivandrum" searches return India-wide
  estimates or Kerala-wide "from" prices. Aestheva shows real, full prices with live free times, which is the angle every
  guide page leads with.
- **Local pack:** "near me" searches are answered first by Google Maps listings (Google Business Profile). Each business
  on Aestheva should have a profile linking to its Aestheva page.
- **Spellings people use:** Trivandrum (most common), Thiruvananthapuram, TVM; Kazhakkoottam / Kazhakuttam / Kazhakoottam;
  "near Technopark".

## Off-site actions (needed to reach the top)
1. Google Search Console: verify bookaestheva.com and submit /sitemap.xml.
2. Google Business Profile for each listed business, with the Aestheva page as the booking link.
3. Reviews from real clients after completed visits.
4. Local links: listings on Justdial, Sulekha and WhatClinic pointing to the Aestheva page; Technopark community and
   resident-association newsletters; Instagram bios of listed artists linking to their Aestheva page.
