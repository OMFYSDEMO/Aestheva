    About these numbers

    • Gross booking value is the list price of every booking that was not cancelled, dated by appointment. No-shows count, because the deposit is kept.
    • Commission earned is 10% of completed marketplace bookings. Direct bookings, which providers bring themselves, carry no commission.
    • Cancellation & no-show rate is the share of all bookings in the period that were cancelled or not attended.
    • On the books is confirmed booking value for the next 14 days. It ignores the period filter.
    • Change compares with the period of the same length just before. Since launch has no comparison.
    • Real data: export bookings in the shape of dashboard/sample-data.json, then run node scripts/build-dashboard.mjs.
